I know you know.

And maybe right now,
you aren't ready for something like this.

That's okay.

Because my feelings were never about rushing you
or asking for more than you can give.

They started with a voice I heard by chance,
grew through a few conversations,
and stayed because of the person you are.

Your beautiful eyes,
your sweet voice,
and the way you unknowingly make my day better.

I won't pretend I don't like you.

I do.

More than I probably should.

But I also respect your pace,
your feelings,
and the time you need.

So for now,
I'll keep admiring you quietly,

grateful that out of all the people I've met,
you're the one my heart chose to write about.
