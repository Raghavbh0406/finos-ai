It's strange how some people become special.

You weren't talking to me that day.

We were standing with our backs facing each other,
and you were talking to someone else.

But then I heard your voice.

Something about that soft little voice of yours
made me want to hear it again.

I didn't know your name,
I didn't know your story,
yet somehow that moment stayed with me.

Later came a few conversations,
a few random calls,
and every day I found another reason to like you.

Your beautiful eyes,
your sweet voice,
and the way you make ordinary moments feel special.

Maybe you'll never know it,
but my story with you began long before we ever talked.

It began the day I heard a voice behind me,
and somehow,
never forgot it.
